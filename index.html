Straight Talk

This Is Not for Everyone

We work with a small number of clients at a time. Before you book a call, be honest about where you are.

This Is for You If...
  • You are doing $1M to $3M in revenue and want to grow without adding more chaos
  • You have a crew that can handle more work and just need the jobs to fill them
  • You are tired of competing on price with contractors half your quality
  • You understand marketing is an investment, not an expense, and you can commit to a minimum 90-day window
  • You respond to leads within the hour and have a sales process in place
  • Your minimum project value is $8,000 or higher
This Is Not for You If...
  • You are under $500K and do not yet have the crew capacity to take on more jobs
  • You want a guaranteed ROI on week one, good marketing takes data, and data takes time
  • You cannot follow up with leads quickly, slow follow-up kills the entire campaign
  • You take every job regardless of size, we build campaigns for specific ticket targets, not volume
  • You have had bad experiences with agencies before but are not willing to approach this differently
  • You are looking for the cheapest option, we are not it, and we are not apologizing for that

The Founder

Built by Someone Who Has Seen Both Sides

I did not come from marketing. I came from watching hardworking people build things with their hands and wondering why the business side never seemed to work in their favor.

Growing up around construction, I saw how talented operators (people who could pour a perfect driveway or build a retaining wall that lasts decades) were constantly fighting for jobs that should have been coming to them naturally. The work was never the problem. The visibility was.

I taught myself paid advertising the hard way: testing, losing money, learning, and rebuilding until I understood what actually moves the needle for service businesses. Not theory. Not tactics from a course. What works in the real world, with real budgets, in real competitive markets.

I started Marketing Genius INC with one focus: hardscaping, paver, and concrete companies between $1M and $3M, operators who have proven they can deliver quality work but have not cracked how to create consistent demand for it. That is the gap I exist to close.
